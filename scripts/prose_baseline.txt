RealRooted/Challenges/Combinatorial/Hoggatt.lean	bracket-display	For positive integers $m$ we set \[ F_{n,m}(t)=\sum_{k=0}^{n-m+1} \left(\prod_{i=0}^{m-1}\binom{n}{k+i}\right)t^k, \] and normalize by its positive constant coefficient to obtain $H_n^{[m]}(t)=F_{n+m-2,m}(t)/F_{n+m-2,m}(0)$ for $n\ge1$. MacMahon's formula identifies the coefficient of $t^k$ with plane partitions in a $k\times(n-1-k)\times m$ box. The checked statements concern the explicit binomial-product polynomials; the combinatorial identification is documentation.

RealRooted/Challenges/PolyaFrequency/LeftProductionMatrix.lean	bracket-display	**Theorem (Pólya frequency Riordan arrays).** Let $d(z)$ and $K(z)$ be power series with Pólya frequency coefficient sequences. The ordinary Riordan array whose $k$th column is $d(z)\bigl(z d(z)K(z)\bigr)^k$ has left production matrix $T(d)\operatorname{diag}(1,T(K))$, with $T$ the lower Toeplitz matrix. Every row polynomial of the array splits over $\mathbb R$ with only nonpositive roots. Series of the form \[ c z^r\frac{\prod_i(1+\alpha_i z)}{\prod_j(1-\beta_jz)}, \qquad c,\alpha_i,\beta_j\geq0, \] with finitely many factors have Pólya frequency coefficients.
RealRooted/Challenges/PolyaFrequency/LeftProductionMatrix.lean	bracket-display	**Theorem (recurrence criterion).** Let $(P_n)$ be a polynomial sequence with \[ \bigl(D_0(z)-xL(z)\bigr)\sum_{n\geq0}P_n(x)z^n=N(z) \] as an identity of coefficients, where $D_0(0)=1$. If $d=N/D_0$ and $K=L/(zN)$ have Pólya frequency coefficient sequences, then every $P_n$ splits over $\mathbb R$ with roots at most zero. Examples are the rows of OEIS A114123 and the polynomials $R_m(x)=\sum_j\binom{m-j}{2j}x^j$.
RealRooted/Challenges/PolyaFrequency/LeftProductionMatrix.lean	bracket-display	Let $Q=(q_{n,k})_{n,k\geq0}$ and let the lower-triangular array $A=(a_{n,k})_{n,k\geq0}$ satisfy \[ a_{n,0}=q_{n,0},\qquad a_{n+1,k+1}=\sum_{j=k}^{n}q_{n+1,j+1}a_{j,k}. \] This is the entrywise form of the left-production factorization $A=Q\,\operatorname{diag}(I_1,Q)\operatorname{diag}(I_2,Q)\cdots$.
