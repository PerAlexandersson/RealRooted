RealRooted/Challenges/PolyaFrequency/LeftProductionMatrix.lean	bracket-display	**Theorem (Pólya frequency Riordan arrays).** Let $d(z)$ and $K(z)$ be power series with Pólya frequency coefficient sequences. The ordinary Riordan array whose $k$th column is $d(z)\bigl(z d(z)K(z)\bigr)^k$ has left production matrix $T(d)\operatorname{diag}(1,T(K))$, with $T$ the lower Toeplitz matrix. Every row polynomial of the array splits over $\mathbb R$ with only nonpositive roots. RealRooted/Challenges/PolyaFrequency/LeftProductionMatrix.lean	bracket-display	**Theorem (recurrence criterion).** Let $(P_n)$ be a polynomial sequence with \[ \bigl(D_0(z)-xL(z)\bigr)\sum_{n\geq0}P_n(x)z^n=N(z) \] as an identity of coefficients, where $D_0(0)=1$.
