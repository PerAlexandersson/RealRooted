# Conventions

## Interlacing

For nonzero real-rooted polynomials, we write $f\ll g$ when their roots alternate,
$g$ has the largest root, and $\deg g-\deg f$ is $0$ or $1$.
Common and repeated roots are allowed.

## The zero polynomial

In closure statements, we allow the zero polynomial as well as real-rooted
polynomials. The zero-aware interlacing relation allows either polynomial to
be zero. A statement that requires nonzero polynomials says so.

## Provenance tags

- **Coauthored paper:** a result from a paper coauthored by Per Alexandersson.
- **RealRooted original:** a result first developed in RealRooted and not
  currently attached to a paper.

## Dates and main results

“Formalized” gives the date the selected main result was first formalized.
“Main result” marks the theorem chosen to represent a page; the declaration
below gives its precise hypotheses and conclusion.

## How to cite

Cite the original mathematical source listed under References.
To cite a formalization, include RealRooted, the declaration name, the GitHub
revision shown on its page, and a link to that page.
